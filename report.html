Community Feedback & Reporting

Seen signs of land subsidence or worsening floods? Share your observations and concerns.

What can you report?

Signs of sinking

Well pipes that seem to rise out of the ground, sinking floors, tilted posts or walls, and doors that no longer close.

Flooding changes

Tides reaching new areas, floods lasting longer, and flooding on days without rain.

Infrastructure damage

Cracks in roads, walls, or bridges, broken drainage, and houses now below road level.
